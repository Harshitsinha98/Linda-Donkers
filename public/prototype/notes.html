Prototype v1

About this prototype

This is a quick, clickable sketch of the new site, so you can try it on your phone before we build it for real. Nothing is booked, paid or saved.

Please try

  1. On the home page, tap Book a massage. Pick a massage and a duration, tap a green day, tap a time, fill in your name and press Pay.
  2. Tap the diamonds on the home page. Do they lead where you expect?
  3. Open Ayurveda travels: is the Panchakarma and Navratri text right?
  4. Open Offers & Christmas: are these the offers you want?

Then tell us: what feels right, what doesn't, what is missing?

What is different from the current site

  • The home page only shows who you are, what you offer, where, and three buttons: book a massage, book a yoga class, contact.
  • Massages and prices are first and most visible.
  • Booking has its own page. The calendar shows free days in green and full days in grey, and the next free time straight away.
  • Booking needs no account. Gift cards and discount codes are behind one small link.
  • Travels: no online booking, only "Book a call", WhatsApp or email.

The real site will be in Dutch and English. This sketch is in English only, to keep it quick.
