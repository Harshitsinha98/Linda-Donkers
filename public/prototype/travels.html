What is Panchakarma?

Panchakarma means "five actions". It is the classic cleansing and renewal treatment of Ayurveda. For two or three weeks you follow a daily programme of warm oil massages, herbal steam, simple healthy food and gentle cleansing treatments. Your body lets go of what it no longer needs and finds its balance again.

You are guided by professional Ayurvedic doctors. Not sure if it suits you? A call with the doctor is possible before you decide.

Ask for a call with the doctor

What is Navratri?

Navratri means "nine nights". It is one of India's biggest festivals, in honour of the Divine Mother. For nine nights there is prayer, music, dance and celebration. During the autumn retreat you live it surrounded by the Himalayan mountains.
